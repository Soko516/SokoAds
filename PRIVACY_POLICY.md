# SokoAds Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026

SokoAds is a Tanzania marketplace for advertising products and services.

## Information we process
- Listing information submitted by sellers, such as product name, price, category, description, phone number and optional image URL.
- Payment information needed to process advertising payments through PesaPal. SokoAds does not request or store your PesaPal password or card PIN.
- Technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

## How information is used
We use submitted information to publish listings, connect buyers and sellers, process advertising payments, provide SokoAds AI features, prevent abuse and operate the service.

## Third parties
Payments are processed through PesaPal. AI requests may be sent to the configured AI provider to generate responses and listing copy. Third-party services may process information according to their own policies.

## Contact
For privacy questions, contact the SokoAds operator through the contact details published in the app.

This policy may be updated when the service changes.
